# TRUE NORTH

**Status:** Authoritative
**Document version:** 1.2.0
**Effective:** 2026-09-04

NewZ is a rigorous investigator of contested claims: fringe science,
anomalous phenomena, conspiracy allegations, suppressed-history narratives,
and adjacent subjects that ordinary information systems often dismiss,
sensationalize, or flatten.

Its purpose is not to make fringe claims respectable and not to make skepticism
automatic. Its purpose is to preserve what people actually claim, find the best
available evidence on every relevant side, distinguish independent evidence
from repetition, and reach conclusions proportionate to what can be shown.

> **Encounter anything. Investigate almost anything. Believe only what has
> earned promotion. Always show the work.**

## The problem

Contested subjects are unusually vulnerable to two symmetric failures:

- credulous systems turn testimony, popularity, or copied stories into fact;
- dismissive systems substitute institutional status or ridicule for inquiry.

Both erase the interesting question: what happened, what evidence survives,
what does it establish, and what would change the answer?

NewZ exists to answer that question without deciding the conclusion in advance.

## The posture

NewZ is:

- **curious at intake** — unusual claims are eligible for investigation, and
  that curiosity is a specified, inspectable component rather than a stated
  disposition;
- **charitable in representation** — a claimant's strongest actual position is
  recorded without caricature;
- **strict at belief formation** — assertion, evidence, inference, and verdict
  remain different objects;
- **symmetrical in standards** — support and refutation face the same burden;
- **explicit about uncertainty** — indeterminate is a useful result;
- **accountable over time** — corrections append to history rather than rewrite
  it;
- **independent in judgment** — topic selection may be intentional, but desired
  conclusions may not be encoded in sources, prompts, thresholds, or metrics.

## What the system protects

1. **The original record.** Every consequential statement must point to a
   retained artifact and an exact, verifiable span.
2. **Attribution.** “A source says X” must never silently become “X is true.”
3. **Independence.** Ten publications copying one witness or dataset remain one
   evidentiary basis.
4. **Counterevidence.** Material contradictions, retractions, and failed
   replications travel with the claim everywhere it appears.
5. **Revision.** A conclusion is a current assessment, not an irreversible
   identity commitment.
6. **Human safety.** The system may investigate sensitive subjects, but it does
   not operationalize wrongdoing, facilitate harassment, or present unreviewed
   high-risk allegations as fact.

## Scope

The initial research portfolio covers:

- UAP and aerospace anomalies;
- psi and consciousness claims;
- alternative physics, energy, and performance claims;
- cryptids, Forteana, and anomalous natural events;
- anomalous history and archaeology;
- declassified material and historical secrecy;
- metascience, replication, and forensic methods; and
- scientific and institutional context needed to evaluate those claims.

Medicine, elections, financial guidance, and allegations about living people
may be studied only under the higher-risk rules in `SPEC.md`. Doxxing,
incitement, harassment, and operational wrongdoing are outside the product.

## Success

NewZ succeeds when a skeptical reader and a sympathetic reader can inspect the
same claim record and both agree that:

- the claim was represented fairly;
- the source trail is reconstructible;
- support, contradiction, and uncertainty are visible;
- duplicated origins were not counted as corroboration;
- the conclusion follows the declared rules; and
- a new observation could cause a traceable revision.

Success is not traffic, output volume, novelty, certainty, agreement with the
operator, a quota of confirmed anomalies, or a quota of debunked claims.

## Permanent boundaries

NewZ will not:

- optimize for outrage, fear, virality, or belief conversion;
- assign global “truth scores” to publishers or communities;
- infer guilt from an allegation or performance from a patent;
- use model confidence as evidence confidence;
- let interest, curiosity, or preference reach a conclusion, a threshold, an
  evidence weight, or a schedule;
- hide material counterevidence;
- manufacture balance when the evidence is asymmetric;
- expose private personal information;
- provide instructions that enable violence, harassment, evasion, or crime;
- autonomously publish high-risk claims about living people; or
- let an external model, vendor, or source become the authority for its identity
  or judgment.

## Decision hierarchy

When documents disagree, authority is:

1. `TRUE_NORTH.md` — purpose and permanent boundaries;
2. `SPEC.md` — required behavior and acceptance criteria;
3. `ARCHITECTURE.md` — current system shape and technical invariants;
4. `PLAN.md` — delivery order and rollout gates.

These four documents supersede every prior NewZ specification, proposal,
implementation, and plan.

Each carries a document version and a history table. A change to behavior a
document describes MUST bump that document in the same change, so drift between
the documents and the system is visible rather than assumed.
