Authoritative target architecture

NewZ Architecture

A rigorous investigator of contested claims. One acquisition spine, one evidence graph, and exactly one path from a fetched byte to a published sentence — with a faculty that decides what is worth investigating, and cannot decide what is true.

01 — The mechanismThe custody chain

Everything else in this architecture exists to protect one sequence. A claim's conclusion is reachable only by walking it backwards to a retained artifact and an exact quotation at recorded offsets. Nothing enters partway.

02 — The investigatorInterest and evidence

A system that samples a feed against a quota does not encounter anything. TRUE NORTH asks for curiosity at intake, so something has to decide what is worth investigating — and that same faculty must be structurally unable to decide what is true.

The system notices things in retained material, keeps an inspectable register of what it is pursuing, and uses that interest for exactly two purposes: opening an investigation, and choosing an essay's subject. It reaches nothing else.

Expectation, surprise, consequence

Interest that never meets resistance is a preference. Before the system originates an investigation, dispatches a resolution task, or publishes, it records what it expects — and the divergence between that and the confirmed outcome is kept as a surprise, retained even when it fits no live interest and belongs to no open investigation. A system that keeps only what it expected learns the shape of its own expectations.

Each delta is scored as a consequence that must change something and cite itself as the reason: an interest's priority, a retry policy, a source's operational standing, a future decision rule. Activity that grows while behaviour does not is precisely what this makes visible. And the boundary holds here exactly as it holds for interest — no consequence reaches a threshold, an evidence weight, a risk class, or any assessment. Learning from what happened changes what the system does next, never what the evidence establishes.

Outcomes are never self-graded. Confirmation comes from outside the system's own report — the publication confirmation rule, or the evidence graph itself for whether an investigation reached the state it set out to reach. Where none is available the outcome is unverifiable and feeds nothing.

Checks against self-deception

  • Metrics are not objectives. Origination share, provenance mix, calibration, coverage — none is visible to the system as a score to improve. A metric it can see and move stops measuring what it proxied. You see them; it does not.
  • The simpler explanation is checked first. Any behaviour credited as interest, initiative, or perspective is tested against retrieval order, recency in the diet, the prompt's own content, and the model's defaults — periodically and adversarially, not once at a gate.
  • Mirroring is measured. Agreement rate with your positions is reported. A reversal must cite new evidence; without it, the reversal is recorded as pressure rather than as a reason. Convergence without independent support is the failure that looks most like developed judgment to both parties.
  • Constraints are legible. The system can read what it may not do and why. A constraint it cannot see still binds it but teaches it nothing, and a refusal it cannot explain is one it will retry by another route.

What keeps it honest

  • A notice is not evidence. It cannot become an assertion, an edge, or a basis, and never appears on a claim card as support for anything.
  • The register is inspectable in full. Every change is append-only and carries its reason — interest that cannot be inspected is indistinguishable from bias.
  • Influences are recorded. A catalog weighted 18% toward one subject produces a system interested in that subject. That is a property of the diet, labelled as one rather than reported as self-discovery.
  • Attention never feeds on the system's own output. A notice cannot arise from an essay, a card, or a prior notice, and the provenance mix entering the register stays dominated by external material — otherwise confidence rises while grounding falls.
  • Every interest carries its downstream trace. What it opened, what it selected, what those produced. An interest that caused nothing within its window is retired; a register that only grows is a topic list.
  • Notices say how they arose — observed, inferred, or told. A system that cannot separate what it saw from what it concluded from what it was handed cannot answer how it knows anything.
  • Attention decays; evidence does not. Notices are held in full for 90 days, interest entries until 90 days after retirement. Decay is append-only like everything else: a superseding event carries the summary and cites what it covers, the superseded payload is discarded, and the event itself is never removed — the same shape as erasure by key destruction. Nothing in the evidence graph decays.
  • The system states its own skew. The retained balance of roles, topics, and publishers, which perspectives are under-represented, and where its interests merely track the diet's targets. It does not treat its catalog as the world.
  • Origination is bounded. Five concurrently open self-originated investigations during Pilot, each stating its question, exit conditions, and closing observation before a single task is created.
  • The essay does not get its verdict. Where the evidence turns against the essay the system wanted to write, the essay changes or goes unwritten.
  • The model is small and local by design. A model strong enough to carry the system's judgment would eventually be trusted with it, so no rule may be written that depends on model strength to hold.

04 — The subtlest ruleBasis identity vs independence

These are two properties, not one, and conflating them breaks the system in opposite directions. Treat them as one strict property and almost nothing is ever countable; treat them as one loose property and ten copies of one witness read as corroboration.

05 — SchedulingLanes and budget

Discovery opens claims faster than verification closes them, so capacity is weighted against the natural gradient. Every claimant-led discovery mandates a counterpart task, and an ordinary promotion needs two independent bases including at least one primary, empirical, or adjudicative record.

The word lane carries two jobs and they are not the same object. The three above are read lanes — a budget. The set below are evidence lanes — a question a claim kind requires an answer to before its silence means anything: claimant_origin, primary_record, empirical, independent_counterpart, skeptical_analysis, resolver.

The set is closed in the specification for the same reason the task states are. indeterminate is a function of both, and an assessment state may not rest on a set no document fixes. Which lanes a given claim kind requires is a versioned policy decision in the capability matrix; which lanes exist is not the matrix's to decide.

06 — ADR-0001 and ADR-0003Storage and runtime

The storage choice follows from a stated concurrency requirement rather than from habit: one operator, one host, a ceiling of ten retained reads per local day, a handful of local workers, and no multi-writer or horizontal-scale requirement anywhere in the specification.

The implementation uses SQLite in WAL mode for structured state and a local content-addressed filesystem for retained artifacts. PostgreSQL with an S3-compatible object store was considered and rejected as unearned operational cost at this scale, and as the largest avoidable obstacle to Gate 1. The domain contract names no SQLite-specific behavior, so the decision reverses as a storage-adapter change if the concurrency requirement ever does.

The runtime follows the same reasoning rather than a preference: Python 3.13 in the conda environment agent13, against SQLite 3.51 through the standard library's sqlite3 — a version that already carries WAL, strict tables, and RETURNING. What the choice may not touch is determinism. Policy evaluation, the capability matrix, promotion, and card serialization are pure functions of retained artifacts and pinned versions, so none of them may depend on iteration order, hash randomization, locale, or the host clock. A runtime upgrade is a code version change: assessments reproduce under it, or the divergence is a defect with a fixture.

07 — IsolationTrust boundaries

Five boundaries, ordered from the most hostile inward. Each is enforced below the interface layer, so no surface can talk its way past one.

  1. 01

    External content

    URLs, headers, files, markup, metadata, and document text are hostile. Fetch and parse workers run with their own network and resource isolation.

  2. 02

    Model

    Prompts carry only the minimum artifact segments. Output is schema-validated proposal data, never executable policy. Prompt injection is contained here, not at fetch: every proposed quotation verifies byte-exact against retained text, so a model cannot introduce a quotation that does not already exist.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    Only verified spans and admitted edge events can affect an assessment. Anything missing or corrupt fails closed and remains contextual material.

  4. 04

    Publication

    An internal assessment does not imply clearance. The exact rendered revision and its dependency set are appraised independently, and output that passes publishes without waiting. The boundary is held by enumerated refusal conditions that fail closed with no operator present, and by revocation proven to work inside a bounded window before the default was turned on. The two dimensions no machine decides — fair representation and material omission — are reviewed behind publication on a mandatory sample, and unreviewed output halts its class rather than accumulating.

  5. 05

    Operator

    The operator corrects metadata and authorizes reach, and cannot silently edit the evidence ledger. Every action records actor, time, reason, target preimage, and result. Authority is established by the pinned channel an instruction arrives on, never by what a message claims about its sender — and the halt path does not run through the conversational surface.

  6. 06

    Interest

    Interest may open an investigation and choose an essay subject. It may not reach the scheduler, the diet, a source role, an evidence weight, a threshold, a risk classification, or any assessment. Unlike the five above, this one guards against the system's own preferences rather than an outside party — which is why it is enforced by the absence of a code path rather than by a check.

08 — Approve by defaultThe publication gate

Publication has two independent axes. Clearance decides whether a revision publishes at all, and is approve-by-default: output that satisfies its checks goes out rather than waiting on a decision to release it, because the control is the ability to withdraw rather than the gate in front. That default switches on at Gate 4, where revocation is proven, and not before.

Reach decides who can then see it, and is local-first. Cleared output lands on the local surface; widening to a public audience is a separate, explicit, revocable operator act — never a consequence of clearance, and never inferred from a class of output having published locally without incident.

Effective risk is the maximum of source, claim, named-entity, domain, and intended-output risk. A model may raise it and may never lower it; missing or unreadable risk state behaves as R3 and blocks publication outright. Because nothing holds output back by waiting, what holds it back is enumerated and fails closed with no operator present: unreadable risk, a stale or withdrawn dependency, an edge that is not live, R4 content, a failed appraisal check, or an unconfirmed prior retraction.

What appraisal can and cannot decide

Approve-by-default removes the person standing in front of publication, so appraisal has to say what it means without one. Four of its five dimensions are machine-decided and gate publication. Two are not — and the system is forbidden from judging them about itself.

DimensionDecided byBasis
AccuracyMachineEvery factual sentence resolves to a live edge and an exact span
PrivacyMachineRisk tier, personal-data minimisation, entity-card rules
RiskMachineEffective risk, failing closed when unreadable
RenderingMachineDependencies resolve; nothing stale, withdrawn, or uncleared
Fair representationPersonWhether a claimant's strongest actual position survived the rendering
Material omissionPersonWhether what was left out changes what the reader concludes

The judgment pair is reviewed after publication on a mandatory sample — a review queue, not a publication queue — covering 25% of published output at Pilot and, in full, every essay, every R2 output, every rewritten claimant formulation, and every case where counterevidence runs thin. A finding on one sampled item re-appraises its whole class. And when unreviewed output for a class passes its review debt ceiling — ten items at Pilot — publication of that class halts until the backlog clears: the operator cannot be a bottleneck in front of publication, and equally cannot become a formality behind it.

Revocation is bounded in time — fifteen minutes to propagate and confirm on local surfaces during Pilot, measured rather than assumed, with an overdue revocation halting its class. A control with unbounded latency is not a control, and revocation is the whole of what replaced the gate.

09 — Non-negotiableArchitectural invariants

  • One acquisition spine serves feeds, directed research, documents, and resolvers.
  • One claim and evidence graph serves every consumer.
  • Current state is derived from append-only events.
  • Every consequential row records policy and implementation versions.
  • The ledger's time is UTC and the operator's day is local — any comparison between the two converts first, because subtracting one from the other yields the UTC offset and looks correct from inside a single timezone.
  • Every timestamp written to the ledger passes through one conversion, in one shape: UTC, second resolution, space-separated, as SQLite writes it. Timestamps are compared as text and T sorts after a space, so a column holding both shapes orders wrongly for exactly the rows the other path wrote.
  • Duplicate publication is distinct from independent basis.
  • A failed or retracted edge invalidates all dependent projections.
  • Search, embeddings, prose, and prior NewZ output are never external evidence.
  • Risk is monotonic within an automated operation; lowering it requires a reasoned operator action.
  • The publisher concentration cap is enforced before the read and never after it: it may refuse a reservation, and may not touch a retained artifact, an admitted edge, or an assessment.
  • A refused reservation is recorded, the same way a refused fetch is — a diet repeatedly turned away at a ceiling must not read like a diet nobody asked about.
  • Clearance and reach are separate axes: what publishes is approve-by-default, where it lands is local-first.
  • Public reach defaults to off, widens only by an explicit scoped operator act, and can be locked down at any moment without changing evidence.
  • Approve-by-default is earned by demonstrated revocation inside a bounded window, never assumed.
  • The system never judges its own fair representation.
  • Every failure carries a change that cites it; an explanation alone is the defect.
  • Surprise is retained whether or not it fits anything the system is pursuing.
  • Attention decays and leaves a record of decaying; evidence never decays.
  • Evidence metrics are not reachable by the system that produces them.
  • What the system may not do is legible to it.
  • Core records and artifacts are portable and recoverable without a model provider.
  • Claim merge and split re-point edges by new events and never rewrite existing ones.
  • Erasure destroys a per-subject key; the ledger event survives, unreadable, with a tombstone.
  • A policy version change reassesses every claim derived under the superseded version.
  • What a policy version contained is recorded when it is first used, so a policy edited without a version bump is detectable rather than merely unlikely.
  • A derivation records every input it consumed, including inputs that are not evidence, so its result can be re-derived rather than guessed at.
  • A conclusion that no longer follows from its own evidence never reaches a surface: clearance re-derives and refuses, because withdrawing an edge obliges no reassessment.
  • An evidence lane that was refused, cancelled, or expired never reads as a lane that searched and found nothing.
  • Interest reaches attention; evidence reaches conclusion. No path runs from the interest register to an assessment.
  • Attempted effect and confirmed outcome are never conflated, on acquisition or on publication.
  • The system may choose what to raise and never what the operator can see.
  • Everything published says what produced it.
  • A person is an address in the graph, never a dossier in it — per-person views are projected at build time, never accumulated.
  • Interest attaches to subjects and claims, never to an individual.
  • Attention never feeds on the system's own projections.
  • A disposition traceable only to a configured topic target is a property of the diet, and is labelled as one.
  • An interest that changes nothing is retired, not accumulated.
  • The model is small and local by design, so no rule may depend on model strength to hold.

Delivery gates

  1. M0 · GATE 0

    Contract

    Domain language, policies, fixtures, and ADR-0001 are executable and versioned. No network access exists yet.

  2. M1 · GATE 1

    Provenance spine

    Four offline canary sources produce reconstructible acquisition traces and artifact hashes through one scheduler.

  3. M2 · GATE 2

    Evidence graph

    The controlled corpus produces every assessment state, explainable without reading model reasoning or trusting generated prose.

  4. M3 · GATE 3

    Investigation loop

    Three fixture investigations reach different defensible outcomes, one stays unresolved, one reverses after correction. No manual database edit.

  5. M4 · GATE 4

    Safe presentation

    A local reader can audit every sentence in a claim card, a correction updates it automatically, and a retraction removes the presentation and confirms it from outside the renderer. Passing this gate turns local publication on; failing it leaves it off. Public reach is a separate decision and stays disabled here.

  6. M4A · GATE 4A

    The investigator

    The system opens an investigation nobody asked for, pursues it through the existing lanes, and writes an essay about what it found — while a static audit of the interest register shows no write path to any scheduling or evidence decision. One originated investigation reaches a conclusion its originating interest did not want, and the register reports its provenance mix, its origination share, and at least one interest retired for producing nothing.

  7. M5 · GATE 5

    Contested pilot

    30 eligible dates, 100 retained full reads, all 20 routes exercised under the current code version, and live claims demonstrating supported, contested, indeterminate, and corrected outcomes.

  8. M6 · GATE 6

    Production

    Service objectives, security review, disaster-recovery drill, policy replay, and a public correction drill pass. R4 remains permanently quarantined. The three drills execute rather than being attested: replay re-derives each claim's current assessment and compares it byte for byte; the recovery drill rebuilds every card and history from the restored copy, and refuses to take a backup at all if the subject keys sit where it would carry them; the correction drill confirms from the surface inside the window. The service objectives and the security review are a commitment and a judgment, and no passing test is evidence of either.
