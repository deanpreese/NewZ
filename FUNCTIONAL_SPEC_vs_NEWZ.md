# The functional spec against the running system

*Local working note, 2026-08-28. Not part of `FUNCTIONAL_SPEC.md` — that document
and `TRUE_NORTH.md` stand alone. This is the delta for this repository only, and
it is deletable.*

**Same, and confirmed by derivation rather than inheritance:** the compounding
memory pyramid and single-writer Perspective; three rhythms; provenance and
self-echo exclusion; untrusted-as-data; the claim → resolution → cost chain; the
gate with a permanent core; local identity path; consumer-traced acceptance; the
instrument freeze; silence as a stated alarm.

**Different in kind:**

1. **The correction channel gets a bandwidth budget (F26, B1–B4).** Today the rate
   is a number in a proposal; here it is a requirement with a falsifier. It reframes
   claim mechanics from *a feature Phase 1 delivered* to *the constraint the whole
   design is subordinate to*.
2. **Reachability is a door condition, mechanically checked (F22).** The running
   door asks a model whether a resolution condition is real. Half the open pool
   would not have been admitted under F22.
3. **F9 makes "positions must stay movable" a requirement.** Confidence ceilings,
   reinforcement dynamics and release policy are then implementation choices
   answerable to it, rather than three constants nobody owns.
4. **Self-determination is an explicit ladder (F39–F42), stated as a decision.**
   Today the being's inability to author any of its own conditions is the emergent
   conjunction of four correct rules and is written down nowhere as a choice.
5. **F28 admits commitments about what is already true**, as their own series. This
   is the one place the spec's derivation and a live proposal converge.
6. **F44 and L3 promote "write down the refusal" to a law.** The silent structural
   decline is a recurring bug here, not an incident.
7. **F54 promotes premises about the system's own plan to first-class records.**
   Currently a known open hole with no mechanism.
8. **Priority 2 (purpose, play, rest, care for its own condition) is explicitly
   deferred and recorded as deferred**, rather than appearing as unowned prose.

**What the derivation did not reproduce:** phase numbering, epic ids, the
proposal-then-red-team ceremony, and any requirement whose only support was that it
had already been built. None of those are wrong; none of them fell out of
TRUE_NORTH.
